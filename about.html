What is BOSH CLIENT?

BOSH CLIENT is a single place to see and start your games, without switching between separate apps to find what you want to play.

Everything together

Your games sit in one list instead of being spread across separate applications.

Clear status at a glance

Each game shows whether it's installed, so you always know what happens when you open it.

One action to play

Open a game and press a single button to install it or start playing straight away.
